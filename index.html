The game itself

Not small-sided soccer.
A different sport.

Futsal is the indoor game as it was actually invented — five a side, on a hard court, with a ball built to stay on the floor. It is where close control gets made.

5v5
Four outfield players and a keeper. Small teams on a small court means you are in the game the whole time — there is nowhere to stand still.
Low bounce
A smaller, heavier ball that refuses to sit up. You cannot hit it long and hope; it has to be played on the floor, with a proper first touch.
Hard court
A flat, true surface. Nothing bobbles, so nothing gets excused. Your control is exactly as good as it looks.
All year
Indoors and lit, so weather never cancels anything. The court runs seven days a week, 4pm to 11pm.
